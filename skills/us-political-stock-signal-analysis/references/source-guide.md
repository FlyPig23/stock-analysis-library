# Source Guide

## Congressional Sources

### Capitol Trades

URL: `https://www.capitoltrades.com`

Use for fast ticker/member search, recent filings, transaction pages, and links to official reports. Treat it as a structured secondary source; verify high-signal records against the linked House or Senate report when possible.

### Quiver Quant Congressional Trading

URL: `https://www.quiverquant.com/congresstrading`

Use for ticker-level and politician-level aggregation. Cross-check transaction type, amount range, and filing date against official PDFs before making high-conviction claims.

### Unusual Whales Politics

URL: `https://unusualwhales.com/politics`

Use for committee/context dashboards and unusual cluster detection when accessible. Verify individual records against official House/Senate disclosures.

### Primary Congressional Sources

- House Clerk public disclosure PTR PDFs
- Senate eFD PTR records
- Official committee and subcommittee rosters
- Hearing calendars, legislation pages, member press releases, and committee reports

## Executive Branch And Trump Sources

### Open Cabinet

URL: `https://open-cabinet.org`

Use for executive-branch officials and OGE Form 278-T transaction reports. Useful pages include Directory, All Trades, Companies, Overview, Late Filings, Methodology, and profile pages.

Open Cabinet states that its data comes from the U.S. Office of Government Ethics and that transaction volume is estimated from reporting-range midpoints. Treat it as a secondary structured source; verify high-stakes conclusions against original OGE PDFs when possible.

### TrumpTrades

URL: `https://trumpstrades.com`

Use for Donald Trump OGE financial disclosure visualization and sorting by transaction, sector, ticker, or top holdings/trades. It is useful for fast exploration of Trump's disclosed trade set, but verify exact records against OGE filings when possible.

### Trump Tracker

URL: `https://trumptracker.org`

Use for browsing Trump administration dashboards, latest trades, and top holdings/assets. It may be better for overview browsing than bulk extraction. Treat it as a discovery layer and cite its pages when used.

### Primary Executive Sources

- OGE public financial disclosure portal
- Original OGE Form 278-T transaction reports and PDFs
- Agency budgets, procurement databases, contract awards, regulatory calendars, and official policy releases

## Market And Catalyst Sources

- Company investor relations pages, SEC filings, earnings releases, and earnings call transcripts
- Agency budgets/contracts, export-control notices, tariff announcements, sanctions, FTC/DOJ actions, rulemaking, and procurement notices
- Reliable market data for price movement around trade date, filing date, and catalyst date

## Validation Cautions

- Public disclosure is delayed; trade date and filing date are different.
- Amounts are usually ranges, not exact values.
- Some records are family accounts, trusts, RSUs, divestitures, compliance sales, or automated transactions.
- ETF/fund/bond/preferred/municipal/structured-note records should not be described as common-stock buys.
- Do not imply illegality. Say "potential conflict", "role-sector overlap", "policy-information advantage", or "requires scrutiny" unless primary evidence supports a stronger claim.
